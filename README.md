# Kairos Connect

KAIROS APP — CHRISTIAN SOCIAL, LIVE STREAMING, BIBLE AI & DIGITAL GIVING PLATFORM

Build a modern, scalable, multi-tenant Christian digital platform called KAIROS APP.

KAIROS APP is a Christian social and ministry platform that connects churches, ministries, pastors, evangelists, Bible teachers and Christians in one digital ecosystem.

The platform should combine:

Christian social networking

Live church services

Digital giving and seed offerings through Paynow

Bible reading and search

AI-powered Bible verse detection

AI-powered related Scripture suggestions

Christian video creation

Background music during recording

Automatic voice/music balancing

AI noise removal

Automatic captions

Sermon transcription

Sermon summaries

Prayer requests

Christian content discovery

Likes, comments, shares and follows

Ministry pages

Events

Analytics

Notifications

The long-term vision is for KAIROS APP to support thousands or potentially millions of users and many independent churches and ministries while keeping each organization's data secure and separate.

The application must therefore be designed as a scalable multi-tenant SaaS/social platform, not as a single-church website.

CONNECT

Follow Christians, pastors and ministries.

GIVE

Give offerings, tithes and seeds securely through Paynow.

DISCOVER

Discover sermons, ministries, Bible teachings and Christian content.

LEARN

Use AI-powered Bible search and Scripture recommendations.

Use the complete feature specification from this project, but replace all references to the generic platform name with KAIROS APP.

Build the application in phases, starting with the database architecture, authentication, profiles, ministries, social feed, Bible system and administration before implementing advanced payments, AI, video processing and live streaming.

Do not pretend advanced integrations are complete if they have not actually been implemented.
